Privacy

Effective 30 August 2026

Review Packet builds packets in your browser.

Files and packet content

The PDF and attachments you select are processed in your browser. Selected files are not stored after a refresh. Packet titles, comments, decisions, and source links are stored in browser local storage to restore your text draft. Demo drafts use a separate local-storage namespace and are removed when you open the empty builder.

Exports

The exported ZIP contains the material you choose. Check the recipients and contents before sharing it.

Analytics and third parties

This site loads no third-party fonts, scripts, advertising, or behavioural analytics. Basic hosting logs and an aggregate privacy-respecting page count may be kept by the host.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator through sociobot.in.
