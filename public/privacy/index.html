Privacy

Effective 28 August 2026

Review Packet is designed so your documents do not need to leave your device.

Files and packet content

The PDF and attachments you select are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to us. The app stores packet titles, comments, decisions, source links, and optional Plus snapshots in your browser’s local storage so your text can survive a refresh. For safety and browser storage limits, selected files are not persisted. You can remove stored text by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

License verification

If you buy or restore Review Packet Plus, your license token and a time-stamped verification result are stored locally. Verification sends the token to the Sociobot billing API. Checkout is provided by Sociobot and Dodo, the merchant of record; their privacy terms apply to purchase information. Review Packet never receives your card details.

Analytics and third parties

This site loads no third-party fonts, scripts, advertising, or behavioural analytics. We do not fingerprint you. The app may be hosted with basic server logs and an aggregate, privacy-respecting page count.

Exports

Exported folders contain the material you chose. Anyone who receives an exported folder can read it. Review Packet does not encrypt exports and is not a legal retention system.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator through sociobot.in.
